About Healthy LC

Hi, I'm Lynzee.

I grew up around sport. As the youngest of ten children, my childhood was spent at (American) football fields, basketball courts and tracks. Sport wasn't something I discovered later in life — it was simply what I knew.

Growing up, my dream wasn't to be an astronaut or a firefighter. I wanted to be an Olympian.

But over time, my understanding of being healthy grew beyond sport and performance. I don't believe being healthy means being skinny, fitting a certain size, following a strict diet or giving up the foods you enjoy.

I believe health is about balance. You can have your treat and live a healthy life too.

Healthy LC exists because I want to share my knowledge with others. I don't believe there is a one-size-fits-all approach. We all have different lives, goals, wants and challenges.

My goal is for you to feel empowered to make healthy lifestyle choices not just while we're working together, but long after you've finished working with me.
