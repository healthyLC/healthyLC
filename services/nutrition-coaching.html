Nutrition Coaching With Healthy LC

As your nutrition coach, I'll help you improve your health, energy and body composition by supporting you to build sustainable food habits.

Rather than simply telling you what to eat, we'll focus on education and behaviour change. I'll help you understand your choices, build habits that work for your lifestyle and provide ongoing accountability along the way.

Your favourite foods don't have to disappear. Instead, we'll look at how they can fit into a balanced way of eating without feeling like you're constantly following rules or restricting yourself.

We'll start by looking at your current habits — including what you eat, when you eat and how those patterns may be affecting things like your energy, performance and body composition.

From there, we'll look at your goals and identify realistic changes you can make. The aim isn't to completely change the way you eat overnight. It's to make Healthy Lifestyle Choices that you can continue long after our coaching ends.

What Does Nutrition Coaching Look Like?

Nutrition coaching will look different for everyone. Your goals, your current habits and the level of support you need will help determine how we work together.

We'll usually start with a food diary. This gives us a picture of what you're currently eating and helps us identify patterns that might be supporting — or getting in the way of — your goals. A food diary can be a useful tool, but it doesn't necessarily need to become a permanent part of your life.

When we look more closely at your nutrition, we'll explore the balance of protein, carbohydrates and fats in your diet. I'll teach you what these macronutrients do, how to track them and how to use that information to make choices that support your goals.

From there, we'll agree on realistic changes to work towards and check in on your progress. How often we check in and the type of support you receive will depend on your individual needs and the coaching package you choose.

As things change, your nutrition coaching can change with you. Something that is useful at the beginning of your journey may not be something you need forever.

No Foods Are Off Limits

I won't offer a cleanse, an elimination diet or a "no carbs after 6pm" rulebook.

You'll learn how to track your macros — and I'll teach you exactly how. Through our check-ins, I can suggest food swaps and practical changes that help move you towards your goals without completely changing the way you live.

You can still eat the family dinner, have the birthday cake and enjoy the Friday glass of wine. We're not trying to create a way of eating that only works when life is perfectly organised.

The goal is to build a food framework you understand, feel confident using and can actually live with — even when life happens.

What Are Your Goals?

There isn't one reason to work with a nutrition coach. You might want to lose weight, change your body composition or reduce measurements around your waist. Or your goals might have very little to do with the number on the scale.

You might simply want to feel better, have more energy, feel more confident around food or finally understand nutrition without all the conflicting information.

Maybe you already know some of the changes you'd like to make but struggle to make them consistently. Or perhaps you don't know where to begin.

Whatever brings you to nutrition coaching, we'll start with your goals and build realistic changes around your life — not expect you to build your life around a diet.

What Happens Next?

If nutrition coaching sounds like something that could help you, the next step is simply to have a conversation.

If you'd like some time to talk through your goals and what you're currently struggling with, book a consultation. If you're not quite sure whether nutrition coaching is right for you, start with a free 15-minute clarity call.
