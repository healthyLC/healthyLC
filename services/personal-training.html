PERSONAL TRAINING

Personal Training That Fits Your Life

Build strength, fitness and confidence with personal training designed around you.

Personal Training With Healthy LC

I'm here to help keep you accountable, motivated and moving towards your goals.

If you want a routine, I'll help you build one specifically for your body, your current fitness level and what you want to achieve.

I'll watch your technique and help you perform exercises safely and effectively, so you can build confidence, make progress and reduce the risk of injury.

Most importantly, we'll work together to create a plan that fits your schedule, your lifestyle and your goals — helping you make Healthy Lifestyle Choices that you can actually maintain.

Where Can We Train?

I'm a travelling and virtual Fitness Coach, offering personal training throughout the Vale of Glamorgan or globally online.

I have the equipment — I'll bring the "gym" to you. All we need is enough space to move.

And don't worry if that means moving a coffee table or a bit of furniture. I can help you move things before your session and put everything back afterwards.

Don't live in the Vale? No trouble. We can work together virtually from the comfort of your own home, or you can take your device with you wherever you want to train.

Virtual coaching can include live training sessions, or I can ask you to record short videos of your movements. This helps keep you accountable while allowing me to give you feedback on your technique and progress.

What Are Your Goals?

Everyone comes to personal training for a different reason. You might want to feel stronger, improve your fitness, move more confidently, build a consistent exercise routine or simply feel better in your body.

You might be completely new to exercise, returning after some time away, or already active and looking for some extra structure, accountability or a new challenge.

That's why I don't start with a one-size-fits-all programme. We'll start with where you are now, talk about what you want to achieve and build your training around you.

What Happens Next?

Not sure where to start? That's completely fine. We can talk about where you are now, what you'd like to achieve and what kind of support would work best for you.

You can book a consultation if you'd like some time to discuss your goals, or if you're ready to get moving, you can book your personal training session.
